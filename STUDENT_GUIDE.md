# Student guide

You can search the catalogue without an account. Use title, author, ISBN, subject or exact accession number. Author and subject filters refine results; **Available only** hides copies that cannot currently be issued. Open a title for publication details and shelf information. Each accession identifies one physical copy, even when several copies share a title.

## Create your account

Choose **Sign in → Create a student account**. Enter your real name, student ID/roll number, programme, department and university email where available (otherwise an email you control). Use a password of at least 12 characters. Follow the verification link sent to your email, then sign in.

Your account initially awaits librarian approval. Verifying your email does not itself grant borrowing access. You can follow your membership status in **My library**. If email does not arrive, check spam and speak with the librarian; the department controls delivery and account approval.

## Request and collect

Once approved, browse an available physical copy and choose **Request this copy**. Follow progress under **My requests**. Pending means the librarian has not decided. Approved means the copy is reserved until the displayed collection deadline. Rejected, cancelled or expired requests do not reserve a copy. A request is not a checkout.

Collect an approved request from the library before its deadline. Bring identification so the librarian can verify your details and issue the exact accession. Only staff can record the physical loan. **My active loans** shows your due dates; the same information appears on your dashboard. Return books physically to the librarian and ensure a check-in is recorded. Your returned books appear under **Borrowing history**.

You can cancel a pending or approved request in **My requests**. Cancellation releases an approved reservation for other readers. Borrowing and request limits are set by the department; approved reservations count toward borrowing limits. Availability can change while you browse, so an unavailable-copy message means you should refresh or choose another copy.

## Profile and account care

**My profile** lets you correct your identity, roll number, programme or department. Changes return your membership to the approval queue. Contact the librarian if you need to change your registered email or resolve an identity discrepancy.

Use **Forgot password?** on sign-in to request a recovery email. Open its link in the same browser where you requested it because the application uses a secure PKCE recovery flow. Choose a new password on the recovery page. Never share passwords with staff or other students. Sign out on shared computers.

Your private requests and borrowing records are visible to you and authorised library staff. Public catalogue visitors cannot see borrower identities or loan history. If you suspect someone else used your account, tell the librarian promptly and reset your password.
